What am I looking at?

Preview version

Leaf area index (LAI)

LAI is the one-sided leaf area per unit ground area (for conifer needles, half their total intercepting area): at an LAI of 4, the leaves above 1 m² of ground, laid flat, would cover it four times. Machine learning models, trained on synthetic stands only, predict the vertical LAI profile of every 2 m × 2 m prediction column: the partial LAI of each 2 m height bin from 0 to 50 m above ground, which sum to the total LAI of the column.

Data

  • Synthetic: stands of The Grove tree meshes with exact LAI labels from the leaf meshes, scanned by HELIOS++ virtual laser scanning matched to the ECOSENSE acquisition. Each is a test stand of the models shown with it; About lists its parameters, such as the twig density (The Grove’s interior twig density) and the simplification (the mesh reduction before scanning: minor, or major for stands with large trees). A filter narrows the list by species, height, foliation and more.
  • ECOSENSE: the real data, UAV LiDAR acquisitions of a mixed forest dominated by European beech on 38 dates from April 2024 to June 2026. Choose one of the 24 reference positions, laid out as in the field (D deciduous, M mixed/coniferous canopy), then a date (H: with HDHP-LAI). Shown are the points of the 109 prediction columns of the canopy cylinder (radius 10.9 m) around the position.

Layers

The chips at the top left switch the layers on and off; the sidebar groups of the same names hold their options.

  • Points: the LiDAR returns, colored by height or another attribute. On the real data, returns less than 1 m above the ground are removed for the models (ground removal) and drawn gray.
  • Partial LAI: one voxel per prediction column and height bin, from a model or, on synthetic stands, the LAI label or an error layer.
  • Total LAI: one cell per prediction column on the ground, by default from the partial LAI source (Follow the partial LAI source).
  • Helpers: Column grid, Markers, Canopy cylinder and Terrain (real data), Axes with the scale bar.

Colors

  • Fading green (default): white to green, the darker, the more LAI; low values turn transparent, so near-empty voxels fade into the background.
  • Viridis: dark purple (little LAI) to yellow (much LAI), chosen under Color map.
  • Prediction − label (synthetic stands): blue where the prediction is below the label, red where it is above. Small errors fade; voxels below 15 % of the color scale are hidden at first.
  • The color scale ends at the 98th percentile of all listed layers (Shared by all layers) or of the drawn layer (Per layer).

Models

Click a model to draw it; its eye shows or hides its line in the profile chart, and the number is its mean total LAI.

  • Gap fraction: the parametric baseline, which requires no training. A Beer–Lambert inversion of the proportion of returns below 1.8 m above the terrain gives one total LAI per canopy cylinder (real data, Total LAI table only).
  • Hand-crafted features: Feature-MLP and Random Forest, on 35 height and density statistics of the points in the prediction column.
  • Raw-point encoders: PointNet++, PointNet and KPConv, on the points of the 6 m × 6 m context window around the column.
  • Fusion: Fusion-A, Fusion-B and Fusion-C, PointNet++ combined with the hand-crafted features.

The trained models were fit on the training partition (36 stands) of the training set (51 synthetic stands, foliation ≤ 75 %). Show side experiments adds models trained without return information or on the training partition (51 stands) of the full stand set (73 stands).

Uncalibrated and calibrated (real data)

Every method predicts from the point cloud alone, so its values are uncalibrated. A model’s value for a reference position and date is its mean LAI over the 109 prediction columns of the canopy cylinder. Calibrated = slope × value + offset, from an ordinary least squares fit of HDHP-LAI on the values of each method over all 600 observations (24 reference positions on 25 dates), including the one shown. The calibrated row (hatched swatch) under each model scales its partial and total LAI by one factor, so the profile keeps its shape; its dashed chart line shows once you select the row or click its eye. Values ≤ 0 are drawn as 0.

Right panel

  • Profile chart: the mean vertical LAI profile over the prediction columns, one line per model. Click a column in the 3D view for its own profile; Esc or Back to the mean returns.
  • Total LAI: the mean total LAI of each model. On the real data Uncalibrated and Calibrated, beside the gap fraction and the field references, interpolated to the date: HDHP-LAI (long-term harmonized DHP LAI, on 25 of the 38 dates) and, in autumn 2024, DHP-LAI (short-term hemispherical-photo LAI) and LT-LAI (short-term litter-trap LAI, deciduous positions only). On synthetic stands, the LAI label, and the bias and mean absolute error of each model against it.
  • About: the stand, or the position, date and acquisition with the data references. The LAI ratio divides the HDHP-LAI of the Low-LAI season (about mid-November to March) by that of the High-LAI season (about May to September).

Transect, views and compare

  • Transect (top left): sliders with two knobs for E, N and H (synthetic stands: x, y, z) cut the scene by prediction column and height bin; set both E knobs to one column for a slice one column deep. On the real data, the height bins of each column start at the lowest DTM elevation of its context window, so a slice follows the terrain. × resets; the chart and the table still cover all columns.
  • Views: from the east, west, north or south (E, W, N, S; synthetic stands: +x, −x, +y, −y), from above or below, or isometric from the front (the start view) or the back, fitted to the transect.
  • Compare (top bar): two layers side by side with one camera; the menu on the left chooses the left layer, the right half shows the active one.

Mouse, touch and keys

  • Rotate left-drag · one finger
  • Pan right-drag, Shift + drag · two fingers
  • Zoom mouse wheel · pinch
  • Values hover; click a column for its profile
  • Orbit center double-click a point
  • Turn click E or N (x, y) of the axis symbol, bottom left, to turn the view by 180°, z to look from below or above
  • 3D view arrows pan, Shift + arrows rotate, + − zoom, Home resets
  • Keys ? help · Esc back to the mean profile · [ ] previous / next date
  • Transect arrows move a knob one step, PgUp PgDn a tenth, Home End to the end

Data references

  • Field LAI: the methods and the data of autumn 2024 (DHP-LAI, LT-LAI), Lotz et al. (2026); the HDHP-LAI series to 2026, the paper.
  • Synthetic stands: The Grove (van Keulen, 2025), growpy (Sperlich & Jaksztat, 2026) and HELIOS++ (Winiwarter et al., 2022).
